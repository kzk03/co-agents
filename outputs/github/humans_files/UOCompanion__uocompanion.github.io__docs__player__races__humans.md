# Humans

Humans are one of the playable races in **Ultima Online**. Until **Mondain’s Legacy**, they were the only playable race; Elves were added with that expansion.

## Quests

You can change races by completing in-game quests (repeatable):

- **Elf → Human:** Complete the trials of the **Human Heritage** quest.  
- **Human → Elf:** Complete the trials of the **Elven Heritage** quest.

## Racial Traits

### Lore
With the return of the Elves, the unique racial abilities of Humans were also revealed—gifts meant to help defend Sosaria from the threats unleashed with **Mondain's Legacy**.

### Strong Back
- Increased carrying capacity beyond what Strength alone allows.  
- **+60 Stones** carry weight.

### Tough
- Faster natural health regeneration.  
- **+2 Hit Point Regeneration**.

### Workhorse
- Improved yields when gathering hides, ore, and lumber.  
- **+1 Logs in Trammel**, **+2 Logs in Felucca**  
- **+1 random Ore** while mining  
- Additional hides when skinning.

### Jack of All Trades
- Basic competence in all skills, even untrained ones.  
- **20.0 minimum** in all skills (does not show on the Skill menu).

!!! note "Note (Publish 64)"
    Jack of All Trades **does not** reduce mana costs for Special Moves. If you don’t have **200+ modified points** across combat skills (Swordsmanship, Mace Fighting, Fencing, Archery, Throwing, Parry, Lumberjacking, Stealth, Poisoning, Bushido, Ninjitsu), Special Moves use **full** mana cost.
